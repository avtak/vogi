# Demo video script (3 minutes)

Record the screen at 1280 × 800 or larger, with the browser zoom at 125 %. Speak slowly.
Use the real model (`muse-spark-1.3`), never the fake host.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | The empty Vogi page | "I learn German in Leipzig. When I ask a chatbot to correct my German, it rewrites everything, and I learn nothing. Or I must write a long prompt first. Vogi needs no prompt." |
| 0:20–0:50 | Type `Gestern ich habe mit mein Bruder Fußball gespielt.` Pick A2 and Grammar only. Click Check. Fix mistake 1 yourself, get mistake 2 wrong once, then right | "I pick my level and what I want: grammar only. Vogi does not rewrite my text. It marks where my mistakes are and gives me a hint. I fix them myself, and only then does it show the rule." |
| 0:50–1:05 | Point at the grey box "Vogi checked its own answer" | "Before I see the answer, Vogi checks it with code: every change must point at words that are really in my text." |
| 1:05–1:40 | Photo tab. Upload the photo of the handwritten text. Fix one misread word. Click "This is what I wrote". Show the red-pen marks | "Most learners write homework by hand. Muse Spark reads my handwriting and copies it with my mistakes. I fix what it misread, and I confirm. Then Vogi marks every mistake with a red pen, right on my page." |
| 1:40–2:05 | Scroll to "My mistakes". Click Practise my top 3. Answer one exercise right and one wrong | "A chatbot forgets my mistakes. Vogi keeps them. After a few texts it knows what I get wrong most, and it trains me on exactly that." |
| 2:05–2:45 | Talk tab, "At the bakery". Speak two lines, end the talk, show the mission ticks and the feedback | "Writing is half of it. In Talk I speak with a baker. She stays at my level and does not interrupt me. Afterwards Vogi marks my spoken mistakes the same way. Muse Voice Transcribe writes down what I said, mistakes included." |
| 2:45–3:00 | The score table, then the repo README | "On my test set, Muse Spark fixed 28 of 28 errors. Everything here was built during the hackathon. Vogi: write and speak German, and learn from your own mistakes." |

The times above leave out the "Sound natural" beat. If the video runs short, add it back.

## Text for the handwritten photo

Write these three sentences by hand on paper, with the mistakes, and photograph them in daylight:

> Gestern ich habe mit mein Bruder Fußball gespielt. Danach wir sind in ein Café gegangen.
> Ich habe ein Kuchen gegessen, weil ich hatte Hunger.

The five mistakes: verb position after *Gestern* and after *Danach*, dative after *mit*,
accusative *einen Kuchen*, and the verb at the end after *weil*.
